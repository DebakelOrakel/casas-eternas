---
title: Arable land
anchor: resource.arable
---

Land where crops grow. A crop needs warm months with enough water, and
the warmer it is, the more water it needs; a season of seven good months
gives a full harvest. Flat land, water on the fields and rain one can
rely on help. Heavy rain in the hot tropics washes the soil out, and a
hot desert has fields only on its rivers and oases. Arable land is one of
the food sources of the carrying capacity, and it changes with the
months.
